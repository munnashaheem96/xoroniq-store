Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to shipping, payments, returns, and order management.

How long does shipping take across India? +
Orders are processed and dispatched within 24-48 hours. Metro cities typically receive deliveries within 2-4 business days, while non-metro locations take 4-7 business days via our express courier partners (Delhivery, BlueDart, Bluedart).
What online payment options are supported? +
We accept all major prepaid digital payment methods securely processed via Razorpay, including UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), Debit & Credit Cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay), and NetBanking.
What is your return & replacement policy? +
We offer a 7-day hassle-free replacement guarantee for damaged, defective, or incorrect items. Simply contact us via email or WhatsApp with a short unboxing video and our team will arrange a reverse pickup.
Are products covered under manufacturer warranty? +
All electronics and smart gear come with a 6-month to 1-year brand warranty. The exact duration is specified in the product specification table.
How can I track my shipment? +
As soon as your order is dispatched, you will receive an SMS and email with your tracking link. You can also track your shipment live at any time on our Live Tracking Page.
