Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

1. Processing & Dispatch

All orders are verified, packed, and dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours from our fulfillment hubs.

2. Delivery Estimates

  • Metro Cities: 3 to 5 business days
  • Rest of India: 4 to 7 business days

3. Shipping Charges

Orders above ₹999 qualify for FREE standard delivery. A nominal shipping fee of ₹49 applies to orders below this amount.

4. Package Tracking

As soon as your package ships, you will receive an SMS and email with your tracking number and courier tracking link.
